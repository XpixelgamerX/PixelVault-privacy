Privacy Policy

How information may be handled when you use PixelVault and its Discord bot features.

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Important: This is a starter policy. Before publishing, make sure it matches PixelVault's actual features, data storage, logs, and third-party services. Remove anything that does not apply and add any information the bot really collects.

01 Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy applies to PixelVault, including its Discord bot and related features (“PixelVault”). It describes information that may be processed when users interact with PixelVault in Discord.

Discord is a separate service with its own privacy policy. PixelVault is not operated by or affiliated with Discord unless explicitly stated otherwise.

02 Information PixelVault may process

Depending on enabled features, PixelVault may process information needed to operate the bot, such as:

  • Discord identifiers: user, server, and channel IDs needed to respond to commands or save server settings.
  • Command data: slash commands, selected options, and information you provide when using a feature.
  • Settings or game data: preferences, balances, inventory, cooldowns, or similar data, if those features exist.
  • Technical logs: error details or basic usage logs, if logging is enabled.
  • Support messages: information you choose to send when asking for help.

PixelVault should never ask for your Discord password or account token. Do not share these secrets with anyone.

03 How information may be used

Information may be used as needed to provide bot commands, save settings or game progress, maintain and troubleshoot the service, prevent abuse, and answer support requests. It should not be used for unrelated purposes without appropriate notice and permission where required.

04 Storage and retention

Some features may require information to be saved in a database or configuration file. The actual information stored, storage provider, and retention period depend on PixelVault's implementation.

Before publishing, state the real retention period. Information should be kept only as long as needed for the stated purpose, subject to legitimate operational, security, or legal needs. Deleting a Discord message may not delete a separate record stored by the bot.

05 Sharing and third-party services

PixelVault should not sell users' personal information. Information may be processed by providers needed to run the bot, such as hosting, database, or AI/API providers, if used. Their own policies may apply.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to address security abuse, comply with applicable law, or protect users and the service. List the actual providers PixelVault uses before publishing.

06 AI and image-generation features

If PixelVault offers AI, image generation, or external API features, prompts, uploaded content, or command options needed for the feature may be sent to the relevant provider for processing. That provider's policies may govern its handling of the information.

If PixelVault does not use these features, remove this section. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information through AI prompts.

07 Your choices and requests

You can stop using PixelVault at any time. If the bot offers commands to clear saved settings or user data, use them as instructed. You may also contact the owner to ask what information is stored or request deletion, where applicable.

Provide privacy requests through the official PixelVault Discord support channel. Add a real support link or contact method before publishing.

08 Security

Reasonable steps should be taken to protect stored information and limit access. However, no online service can be guaranteed completely secure. Never publish API keys, bot tokens, database credentials, or other secrets in a public repository.

09 Children's privacy

PixelVault is not intended to collect more personal information than necessary to provide its features. Users should follow Discord's minimum-age requirements and the laws that apply where they live. If you believe information has been collected inappropriately, contact the PixelVault owner.

10 Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when PixelVault's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a new version is published.

11 Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the PixelVault owner through the official PixelVault Discord support channel.
